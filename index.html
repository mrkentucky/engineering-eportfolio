01 · ABOUT

A broad engineering foundation with a growing focus on intelligent systems.

I am a final-year Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Cape Town. My degree has given me a broad foundation across mechanical systems, electronics, control, programming and system design.

I wanted to be Iron Man. Life said: "The best I can give you is Spider-Man without the superpowers. You're a smart enough kid, but you're no billionaire."

So I settled for engineering — building the things Tony Stark would probably outsource anyway.

What interests me most is where these disciplines meet: systems that can sense, process information and respond intelligently. My project work has taken me from physical prototypes and control systems to computer vision and machine learning.

I enjoy taking an open-ended engineering problem, breaking it into smaller parts, modelling possible solutions and testing whether the solution actually works.
