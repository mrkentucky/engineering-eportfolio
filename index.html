01 · ABOUT

More than a collection of projects.

I am a final-year Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Cape Town. My degree has given me a broad engineering foundation across mechanical systems, electronics, control, programming and system design.

What interests me most is the point where these disciplines meet: systems that can sense their environment, make decisions and respond intelligently. My recent work has therefore moved towards robotics, computer vision, machine learning and quantitative analysis.

I enjoy taking an open-ended engineering problem, breaking it into smaller parts, modelling possible solutions and then testing whether the solution actually works.
